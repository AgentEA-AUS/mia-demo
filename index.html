Meet Mia, your AI receptionist

Pop in your name and mobile. Mia will ring you within a few seconds. Talk to her the way a caller would.

Things to try

  • "I'd like to book an inspection in Euroa." She takes your details and puts you through to the right agent. In this demo, Jonathon's phone rings in place of your team.
  • "I'm renting in Seymour and a pipe's burst." She treats it as urgent maintenance and gets the property manager.
  • "Can I speak to Shane?" She takes a message, unless you tell her Shane is expecting your call.
  • "Are you a robot?" She always tells the truth.

Mia calls from a US trial number, +1 606 755 3008. At go-live she answers your own office lines. Calls are recorded and transcribed so we can review and improve her.
